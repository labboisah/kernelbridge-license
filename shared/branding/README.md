# Shared KernelBridge Licensing Brand Assets

`kernelbridge-logo.png` and `activation.css` are the canonical package-owned activation-screen assets. The logo and visual identity must remain visible in every supported package's activation and licensing screens; products may show their own name as contextual product information but cannot replace or white-label KernelBridge.

Packages may copy these assets into their published artifacts as part of their build/release process. Keep package copies synchronized with these shared files and validate them in the activation UI before releasing.
